# GDPR Compliance Checklist

Use this checklist in Step 4 of the audit. Mark each item:

- ✅ Covered adequately.
- ⚠️ Partially covered or vague.
- ❌ Missing or incorrect.
- N/A Not applicable to this application.

> Do not mark an item ❌ only because no implementation was found in the code. Use "not verifiable"
> when the source cannot confirm or deny the practice. This checklist assesses the legal
> documentation and, where available, the implementation.

## Documentary-quality items (best practice, not legal obligations)

Track these separately in the "Minor findings" section:

- Contact details and DPO information are current.
- Documents include a version or last-updated date.
- A plain-language summary or layered notice is provided.
- Terms of Service reflect the current feature set.
- Terminology is consistent across documents.
- The privacy policy is reachable from the app and from the store listings used.
